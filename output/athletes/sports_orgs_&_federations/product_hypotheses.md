## Hypotheses

### 1. Nexus Core
**Problem:** Fragmented data silos across scouting, medical, and financial departments leading to high-cost injury risks and administrative friction.

**Value Prop:** A unified command center for institutional 'Whales' that integrates disparate APIs into a single source of truth for executive decision-making.

**Key Features:**
- Cross-departmental API Data Aggregator
- Automated GDPR & CCPA Compliance Engine
- Executive ROI Dashboard for Tech Investments

---

### 2. ContractGuard AI
**Problem:** Inaccurate player valuations and preventable 'sunk costs' due to hidden injury patterns and performance volatility.

**Value Prop:** Predictive 'Win-Loss' modeling that optimizes contract negotiations by quantifying the financial risk of every player acquisition.

**Key Features:**
- AI-Driven 'Total Cost of Ownership' Player Projection
- Injury-to-Contract Correlation Engine
- Real-time Market Valuation Benchmarking

---

### 3. WhaleSight Agent
**Problem:** Human cognitive limits in processing global scouting data, resulting in missed opportunities and delayed strategic pivots.

**Value Prop:** Autonomous Agentic AI that scans global datasets 24/7 to predictively scout talent and simulate roster scenarios before competitors act.

**Key Features:**
- Agentic AI Predictive Scouting Scanners
- Automated Roster Stress-Test Simulations
- Natural Language 'What-If' Strategy Interface
